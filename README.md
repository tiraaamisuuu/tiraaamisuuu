# Alfie Corthine

I'm a **Computer Science student at Royal Holloway, University of London**, with a particular interest in **algorithms, artificial intelligence, mathematics and systems programming**.

I'm fascinated by how complex behaviour can emerge from simple rules, whether through evolutionary algorithms, machine learning, or computational systems. I enjoy exploring the mathematics behind computing just as much as implementing the ideas themselves.

Beyond that, I enjoy experimenting with **Linux, cybersecurity, self-hosted software and game development**. I'm particularly drawn to projects that challenge my understanding of how things work.
