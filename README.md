# Alfie Corthine

**Computer Science undergraduate at Royal Holloway, University of London.**

I'm interested in the **mathematical foundations of computation, artificial intelligence and emerging computing paradigms**, particularly how new algorithms, architectures and computational methods can push the boundaries of what's possible.

My interests span **evolutionary computation, biologically inspired intelligence, quantum computing, photonic computing and optimisation**. I'm fascinated by how complex behaviour can emerge from simple rules, how intelligent systems can learn and adapt, and how unconventional approaches to computation might accelerate scientific and technological progress.

I enjoy exploring ideas from both theoretical and practical perspectives, from understanding their mathematical foundations to implementing and experimenting with them.

### Projects & Exploration

My main ongoing project is a **chess engine developed from scratch in C++**, exploring search algorithms, neural network evaluation and computational performance.

Beyond this, I experiment with **systems programming, Linux, cybersecurity, self-hosted infrastructure and game development**.

I'm also beginning to explore independent research, with the aim of investigating computational methods through mathematical analysis, experimentation and reproducible implementations.

### Research Interests

- **Artificial Intelligence:** Machine learning, evolutionary algorithms, biologically inspired computation and emergent intelligence.
- **Unconventional Computing:** Quantum computing, photonic computing and alternative computational architectures.
- **Theoretical Computer Science:** Algorithms, computational complexity, optimisation and mathematical foundations.
- **Scientific Computing:** Computational modelling, simulation and methods for accelerating scientific discovery.
